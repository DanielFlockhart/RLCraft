Minecraft 1.18.1 Completion Guide
=================================

This guide breaks down each individual step for completing Minecraft in version 1.18.1.

"Completing the game" usually means defeating the Ender Dragon and entering the End exit portal to see the credits.

This route is written for a safe, casual Survival playthrough rather than a risky speedrun.


0. Start the world correctly
----------------------------

1. Create or load a Survival world.
2. Play on Easy, Normal, or Hard.
3. Do not use Peaceful if your goal is to complete the game normally, because you need hostile mob drops like blaze rods.
4. Optional but useful: enable coordinates.
5. In Java Edition, press F3 to view coordinates.
6. In Bedrock Edition, enable coordinates in world settings.
7. Your final goal chain is:

   Overworld gear -> Nether portal -> Nether fortress -> blaze rods -> ender pearls -> Eyes of Ender -> stronghold -> End portal -> Ender Dragon fight -> credits


1. Survive the first day
------------------------

8. Punch a tree until you get at least 4 logs.
9. Open your inventory and turn logs into wooden planks.
10. Craft a crafting table.
11. Craft sticks.
12. Craft a wooden pickaxe.
13. Find exposed stone or dig into a hillside.
14. Mine at least 11 cobblestone.
15. Craft these stone tools:

    - Stone pickaxe
    - Stone axe
    - Stone sword
    - Furnace

16. Kill passive animals such as cows, pigs, sheep, or chickens for food.
17. If you find sheep, collect 3 wool of the same color.
18. Craft a bed using:

    - 3 wool
    - 3 planks

19. Collect extra wood. Aim for around 20-30 logs before leaving your starting area.
20. Find coal, or make charcoal by smelting logs in a furnace.
21. Craft torches.
22. Before night, either sleep in a bed, build a small shelter, or stay underground and mine safely.


2. Get basic supplies
---------------------

23. Keep collecting food until you have at least one stack or near-stack of cooked food.
24. Smelt raw food in a furnace.
25. Collect more cobblestone.
26. Craft a second furnace if needed.
27. Make more torches.
28. Collect some dirt, cobblestone, or deepslate blocks. You will later need blocks for building in the Nether and the End.
29. Look for a village if one is nearby.
30. Villages can provide beds, food, hay bales, iron golems, chests, and villagers for trading.
31. If you find hay bales, break them and craft them into wheat, then bread.
32. If you find a village, take a few beds. Beds are useful for sleeping and can also be used later as an optional dragon-fighting tactic.


3. Get iron
-----------

33. Look for a cave, mountain, or exposed stone area.
34. Mine iron ore when you find it.
35. In Minecraft 1.18.1, iron is common in caves and also appears heavily in mountains.
36. Smelt raw iron in a furnace.
37. Your first iron item should usually be a shield.
38. Shield recipe:

    - 1 iron ingot
    - 6 planks

39. Equip the shield in your off-hand.
40. Make an iron pickaxe.
41. Make a bucket.
42. Fill the bucket with water.
43. Make iron armor when you have enough iron.
44. Recommended minimum iron gear:

    - Shield
    - Iron pickaxe
    - Water bucket
    - Iron sword or axe
    - At least some iron armor

45. Ideal safe gear:

    - Full iron armor
    - Iron sword
    - Iron pickaxe
    - Shield
    - Water bucket
    - Bow
    - Food
    - Torches


4. Mine for better gear
-----------------------

46. Decide whether you want to beat the game with iron or get diamonds first.
47. You can beat Minecraft with iron gear, but diamonds make it safer.
48. In version 1.18.1, the best diamond levels are deep underground, commonly around Y = -58.
49. Dig down safely using a staircase. Never dig straight down.
50. Bring food, torches, pickaxes, a shield, a water bucket, and blocks.
51. Mine carefully around lava.
52. Use your water bucket to turn lava into obsidian or to save yourself from falls.
53. Mine diamonds with an iron pickaxe or better.
54. If you get at least 3 diamonds, craft a diamond pickaxe.
55. If you get more diamonds, consider crafting:

    - Diamond sword
    - Diamond chestplate
    - Diamond boots
    - Enchanting table, if you also have obsidian and a book

56. Diamond armor is helpful but not required.


5. Prepare for the Nether
-------------------------

57. To enter the Nether, you need a Nether portal.
58. A Nether portal frame requires at least 10 obsidian.
59. You can get obsidian in two main ways:

    - Mine obsidian with a diamond pickaxe
    - Build a portal using lava and water

60. If using a diamond pickaxe, find lava and pour water over it to create obsidian.
61. Mine at least 10 obsidian.
62. Build a portal frame:

    - 4 blocks wide
    - 5 blocks tall
    - Corners optional

63. Craft or find flint and steel.
64. Flint and steel recipe:

    - 1 iron ingot
    - 1 flint

65. Light the portal with flint and steel.
66. Before entering the Nether, prepare carefully.


6. Nether preparation checklist
-------------------------------

Bring these before going into the Nether:

67. Full iron armor or better.
68. Shield.
69. Iron or diamond sword.
70. Bow and arrows, if possible.
71. Pickaxe.
72. At least one stack of blocks.
73. At least one stack of food.
74. Torches, though they are less useful in the Nether.
75. Gold armor piece, ideally gold boots.
76. Flint and steel.
77. Crafting table.
78. Optional: spare iron.
79. Optional: boat, useful for trapping Endermen later.
80. Optional: extra obsidian, in case you need an emergency portal.
81. Wear at least one piece of gold armor so Piglins do not attack you immediately.


7. Enter the Nether
-------------------

82. Step through the portal.
83. Immediately check your surroundings.
84. Protect the portal area with blocks if it is exposed.
85. Mark the coordinates of your portal.
86. Build a small shelter around your Nether portal if needed.
87. Avoid falling into lava.
88. Watch for Ghasts, Piglins, Hoglins, Skeletons, and lava pockets.
89. Your main Nether goal is to find a Nether Fortress.
90. A secondary goal is to get ender pearls, usually from Piglin bartering or killing Endermen.


8. Find a Nether Fortress
-------------------------

91. Explore carefully from your portal.
92. Use blocks to bridge over gaps, but crouch while bridging.
93. Look for a large dark red brick structure. That is a Nether Fortress.
94. Nether Fortresses often generate in long bridge-like sections.
95. Keep track of your path so you can return to your portal.
96. Use torches, blocks, or coordinates to mark where you have been.
97. Avoid fighting too many mobs at once.
98. When you find the fortress, enter carefully.


9. Get blaze rods
-----------------

99. Inside the Nether Fortress, look for Blazes.
100. Blazes are yellow flying mobs that shoot fireballs.
101. Use your shield and cover to block their fire attacks.
102. A bow can kill Blazes from a distance.
103. A sword or axe works if you can safely get close.
104. Look for a Blaze spawner. It looks like a cage with a spinning Blaze inside.
105. Build partial walls around the Blaze spawner area to make farming safer.
106. Kill Blazes until you collect blaze rods.
107. You need blaze rods to make blaze powder.
108. Each blaze rod crafts into 2 blaze powder.
109. Each Eye of Ender requires:

     - 1 ender pearl
     - 1 blaze powder

110. The absolute minimum for a full empty End portal is 12 Eyes of Ender.
111. That means 12 blaze powder, which means 6 blaze rods.
112. However, Eyes of Ender can break when thrown, and some may be needed to locate the stronghold.
113. A safer target is 8-10 blaze rods.
114. If you want a brewing stand, collect at least one extra blaze rod.
115. Leave the fortress once you have enough blaze rods.


10. Get ender pearls
--------------------

You need ender pearls to craft Eyes of Ender.

Method A: Kill Endermen

116. Endermen spawn in the Overworld at night, in caves, in the Nether, and heavily in Warped Forest biomes.
117. Look at an Enderman to make it angry.
118. Stand under a 2-block-high roof so it cannot reach you easily.
119. Hit it with your sword or axe.
120. Repeat until it drops an ender pearl.
121. This is slower but straightforward.

Method B: Piglin bartering

122. Mine gold ore or Nether gold ore.
123. Smelt gold ore if necessary.
124. Craft gold ingots.
125. Find Piglins in the Nether.
126. Wear one piece of gold armor so they are not hostile by default.
127. Drop gold ingots near Piglins.
128. They may give random items in exchange.
129. One possible barter result is ender pearls.
130. This method can be faster if you have lots of gold.
131. Be careful: Piglin Brutes in Bastions are always hostile and are very dangerous.

Method C: Villager trading

132. Find or create a Cleric villager.
133. A Cleric can eventually sell ender pearls.
134. This requires villager leveling and emeralds, so it is slower but safe.
135. For most playthroughs, killing Endermen or Piglin bartering is simpler.


11. Craft Eyes of Ender
-----------------------

136. Return safely to the Overworld.
137. Craft blaze rods into blaze powder.
138. Combine:

     - 1 blaze powder
     - 1 ender pearl

139. This creates 1 Eye of Ender.
140. Aim to have at least 12 Eyes of Ender.
141. A safer number is 14-18 Eyes of Ender.
142. Bring extra ender pearls if you have them.


12. Optional but strongly recommended upgrades
---------------------------------------------

Enchanting

143. Craft an enchanting table if you have:

     - 4 obsidian
     - 2 diamonds
     - 1 book

144. Books require paper and leather.
145. Paper comes from sugar cane.
146. Leather comes from cows, horses, llamas, or other sources.
147. Place bookshelves around the enchanting table for better enchantments.
148. Useful enchantments include:

     - Protection on armor
     - Feather Falling on boots
     - Power on bow
     - Sharpness on sword
     - Unbreaking
     - Efficiency on pickaxe

149. Feather Falling is especially useful because the dragon can launch you into the air.

Brewing

150. Craft a brewing stand using:

     - 1 blaze rod
     - 3 cobblestone or blackstone

151. Brew helpful potions if you want extra safety.
152. Useful potions include:

     - Slow Falling
     - Strength
     - Regeneration
     - Healing
     - Fire Resistance

153. Slow Falling is one of the best potions for the Ender Dragon fight.
154. To make Slow Falling, you need a phantom membrane.
155. Phantoms spawn if you do not sleep for several in-game days.
156. Kill phantoms and collect phantom membranes.
157. Brew Slow Falling using:

     - Water bottle
     - Nether wart
     - Phantom membrane
     - Optional redstone to extend duration

Golden apples

158. Mine gold.
159. Collect apples by breaking oak or dark oak leaves.
160. Craft golden apples with:

     - 1 apple
     - 8 gold ingots

161. Bring 2-5 golden apples if possible.


13. Final stronghold preparation checklist
------------------------------------------

Before searching for the stronghold, bring:

162. Eyes of Ender, ideally 14-18.
163. Full iron armor or better.
164. Shield.
165. Sword or axe.
166. Bow.
167. At least 1 stack of arrows, or an Infinity bow.
168. Pickaxe.
169. Water bucket.
170. At least 2 stacks of blocks.
171. Food.
172. Torches.
173. Bed, for setting spawn near the stronghold.
174. Crafting table.
175. Furnace.
176. Extra wood.
177. Optional: Ender pearls.
178. Optional: Slow Falling potions.
179. Optional: golden apples.
180. Optional: carved pumpkin.
181. Optional: spare armor or shield.


14. Locate the stronghold
-------------------------

182. Go to an open area in the Overworld.
183. Hold an Eye of Ender.
184. Right-click or use it.
185. The Eye will fly toward the nearest stronghold.
186. Follow the direction it flies.
187. Pick the Eye back up if it drops.
188. Sometimes the Eye breaks. This is why you brought extras.
189. Travel a few hundred blocks in the direction the Eye points.
190. Throw another Eye.
191. Repeat this process.
192. When the Eye starts going downward or backward, you are close.
193. Stand near the point where the Eye drops or dives.
194. Dig down carefully using a staircase, not straight down.
195. Listen for stone brick sounds, silverfish, or cave noises.
196. Eventually, you should enter the stronghold.


15. Explore the stronghold
--------------------------

197. Place torches to mark where you have been.
198. Block off dangerous paths if needed.
199. Watch for silverfish.
200. Search for the End Portal Room.
201. Strongholds are maze-like, so move systematically.
202. Useful stronghold rooms include:

     - Libraries
     - Chests
     - Staircases
     - Corridors
     - Fountains
     - Jail rooms
     - End Portal Room

203. Libraries can contain books and enchanted books.
204. The End Portal Room has:

     - Lava below the portal
     - A silverfish spawner
     - 12 portal frame blocks

205. When you find the portal room, kill nearby silverfish.
206. Break or disable the silverfish spawner.
207. Block off the lava if needed.
208. Place a bed somewhere safe near the portal room.
209. Sleep in the bed or right-click it to set your spawn.
210. Place a chest nearby if you want to store extra supplies.


16. Activate the End Portal
---------------------------

211. Look at the End Portal frames.
212. Some frames may already contain Eyes of Ender.
213. Fill the empty frames with Eyes of Ender.
214. There are 12 frames total.
215. Once all frames are filled, the End Portal activates.
216. The portal appears as a dark starry square.
217. Do not jump in until you are fully ready.


17. Final End fight checklist
-----------------------------

Before entering the End, make sure you have:

218. Armor equipped.
219. Shield equipped.
220. Sword or axe.
221. Bow and arrows.
222. Pickaxe.
223. Water bucket.
224. Blocks.
225. Food.
226. Slow Falling potion if available.
227. Golden apples if available.
228. Ender pearls if available.
229. Carved pumpkin if you want to avoid angering Endermen.
230. Empty inventory space for loot is not necessary, but helpful.
231. Set your spawn near the portal.
232. Jump into the End Portal.


18. Enter the End
-----------------

233. You will spawn on an obsidian platform.
234. Sometimes the platform is away from the main End island.
235. If you spawn away from the island, carefully bridge toward it.
236. Crouch while bridging.
237. If the platform is inside or under the island, dig upward or sideways carefully.
238. Once on the main island, avoid looking directly at Endermen.
239. Place water if Endermen attack you. Endermen avoid water.
240. Your first goal is to destroy the End crystals.


19. Understand the Ender Dragon fight
-------------------------------------

241. The dragon is healed by End crystals on top of obsidian pillars.
242. You need to destroy the crystals first.
243. Some crystals are exposed.
244. Some crystals are protected by iron bars.
245. The dragon flies around the island.
246. The dragon periodically lands at the central bedrock fountain.
247. When the dragon lands, you can attack it with melee weapons.
248. The dragon can launch you into the air.
249. This is why Slow Falling, Feather Falling, water buckets, or ender pearls are useful.


20. Destroy the End crystals
----------------------------

250. Use your bow to shoot exposed End crystals.
251. Aim carefully at the crystals on top of the obsidian pillars.
252. When a crystal breaks, it explodes.
253. Do not stand close to crystals when breaking them.
254. For caged crystals, climb the obsidian pillar or pillar up with blocks.
255. Bring blocks and your pickaxe.
256. Break the iron bars around the crystal.
257. Move away from the crystal.
258. Shoot it or hit it from a safe distance.
259. Climb down carefully.
260. Repeat until all End crystals are destroyed.
261. You can also use water to climb down safely.
262. If the dragon knocks you off a tower, use:

     - Water bucket
     - Ender pearl
     - Slow Falling
     - Blocks
     - Luck

263. Once all crystals are destroyed, the dragon can no longer heal.


21. Fight the Ender Dragon
--------------------------

264. Shoot the dragon with a bow while it flies.
265. Watch its movement and lead your shots slightly.
266. When the dragon lands at the center fountain, move in.
267. Do not stand directly in front of its head for too long.
268. Hit it with your sword or axe.
269. Critical hits help if you can jump safely.
270. Back away when the dragon prepares to leave.
271. Avoid the purple dragon breath on the ground.
272. Keep eating food to maintain health and saturation.
273. Use golden apples if you get low.
274. Use water if Endermen attack you.
275. Repeat the cycle:

     Shoot while flying -> melee while perched -> heal when needed

276. Continue until the dragon reaches zero health.


22. Defeat the dragon
---------------------

277. When the dragon dies, it flies to the center fountain.
278. It explodes in a large death animation.
279. It drops a huge amount of experience.
280. The End exit portal activates at the center.
281. A dragon egg appears on top of the portal structure.
282. You have now beaten the main objective of Minecraft.


23. Enter the exit portal
-------------------------

283. Walk into the End exit portal.
284. You will see the Minecraft credits.
285. After the credits, you return to your spawn point or bed.
286. At this point, the game is considered completed.


24. Optional: collect the dragon egg
------------------------------------

The dragon egg is a trophy. It is optional.

287. Do not mine the dragon egg directly. It will teleport.
288. Click or hit it once so it teleports to nearby End stone.
289. Find where it teleported.
290. Dig two blocks underneath it.
291. Place a torch below the block the egg is sitting on.
292. Break the block under the egg.
293. The egg falls onto the torch and drops as an item.
294. Pick up the dragon egg.


25. Optional post-game goals
----------------------------

295. Look for the End gateway portal that appears near the main island.
296. Throw an ender pearl into the gateway.
297. This takes you to the outer End islands.
298. Search for End Cities.
299. Find an End Ship.
300. Collect Elytra from the End Ship.
301. Collect shulker shells from Shulkers.
302. Craft shulker boxes.
303. Return home using another gateway, or by entering the central End exit portal again.
304. Upgrade your gear.
305. Build farms.
306. Respawn the dragon if desired using End crystals.


Recommended minimum gear for a first-time safe completion
---------------------------------------------------------

For a comfortable Minecraft 1.18.1 dragon fight, aim for:

- Full iron armor or better
- Shield
- Iron or diamond sword
- Bow with at least 64 arrows
- Iron or diamond pickaxe
- Water bucket
- 2 stacks of blocks
- 1 stack of cooked food
- 14-18 Eyes of Ender
- A bed set near the End Portal
- Optional but very helpful: Slow Falling potions
- Optional: golden apples
- Optional: Feather Falling boots


Simplest objective summary
--------------------------

1. Get wood.
2. Get stone tools.
3. Get food.
4. Get a bed.
5. Get iron.
6. Make shield, bucket, armor, and pickaxe.
7. Get diamonds if you want safer gear.
8. Build a Nether portal.
9. Find a Nether Fortress.
10. Kill Blazes.
11. Collect blaze rods.
12. Get ender pearls.
13. Craft Eyes of Ender.
14. Follow Eyes of Ender to the stronghold.
15. Find the End Portal room.
16. Fill the portal frames with Eyes of Ender.
17. Enter the End.
18. Destroy End crystals.
19. Kill the Ender Dragon.
20. Enter the exit portal.

That is the full main-game completion route for Minecraft 1.18.1.
